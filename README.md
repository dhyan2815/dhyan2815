## About

B.Tech in Computer Engineering (GPA 8.75/10). I build applied AI systems end to end: computer vision and predictive models, LLM-backed products, and the automation that connects them to real business processes. Based in Gandhinagar, Gujarat (IST).

## Currently working on

- **LinearCard** — a digital-wallet loyalty pass platform that I'm building solo as an AI/ML developer intern at Linearloop. Brands design, issue, and sell passes; Google Wallet integration is live.
- **SmokeSignal AI** — retraining the wildfire detector to cut false positives on edge cases such as sunrise/sunset hues and dark cloud formations. Targets: false positive rate under 5%, overall accuracy at or above 94%.
